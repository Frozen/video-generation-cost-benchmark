# Sampled-frame visual inspection

Method: assistant inspection of five sampled frames per original clip, with model identities visible. All twenty preregistered pairs were inspected. This is an **unblinded visual screen**, not the frozen human video-and-audio review, not an audio assessment and not proof of temporal coherence. The contact sheets are provided for verification; watch full clips before assigning quality grades. No aggregate winner or acceptance rate is inferred.

The clearest visible differences concern prompt coverage and framing: LTX's dance sample crops the head despite a full-body request; fal keeps the full dancer visible. The coffee sample from fal visibly exposes a heart-like milk pattern; the sampled LTX frames do not clearly show the requested pattern. The OPEN text is much larger and readily legible in LTX's bakery sample. These observations are specific to the selected prompt/seed outputs, not model-wide performance claims.
